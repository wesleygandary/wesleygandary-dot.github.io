01 — About

Purpose in motion.

Wesley Thabiso Gandary is a Zimbabwean creative entrepreneur, youth advocate, arts and media practitioner, and author. He is the Founder & Executive Director of Gandary Visions, Vision Arts Foundation, and Visions Magazine.

His work sits at the intersection of creativity, entrepreneurship, youth development, arts, media and community impact — turning ideas into platforms, opportunities and meaningful work.
